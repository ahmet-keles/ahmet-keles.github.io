K-Map Looper

A drawing tool only — it doesn't simplify anything. You fill the cells and pick the groups; it draws the loops cleanly so you can paste the image into your submission.

Map

Mode

Click a cell to cycle 0 → 1 → blank.

Click cells to add/remove them from the current loop. Wrap-around groups (top↔bottom, left↔right) are drawn as open-ended pieces automatically.

Export

In Pages: paste (⌘V) after Copy, or drag the shown image into the document. If neither works here, a screenshot (⇧⌘4) of the map is equally fine.

Right-click → Copy Image / Save Image, or drag it into Pages.
